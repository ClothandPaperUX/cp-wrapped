Before you start

  1. Export orders from Shopify: Orders → filter Date created Jan 1 – Nov 30 → Export → All orders matching your search → CSV. Shopify emails a zip if it's big. Unzip it and keep every file.
  2. Get the wholesale list: Customers → filter by tag wholesale (and faire) → Export. Copy the Email column.
  3. Don't open and re-save the order files in Excel first. Excel changes the dates.
